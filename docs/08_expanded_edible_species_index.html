08. Master Index: 50+ Wild Edible Mushrooms of Finland

Suomen 50+ Syötävää Luonnonsientä • 芬兰50+常见食用野生蘑菇全景图谱

Finland’s pristine taiga and hemiboreal forests host over 50 commercially approved and traditional wild edible mushroom species (kauppasienet ja perinteiset ruokasienet). While novices should always begin with the "Safe Five", intermediate and seasoned foragers can explore this definitive taxonomic index of 54 edible species found across Southern Finland and Uusimaa.

🧭 How to Read the Edibility Codes

  • *** Choice Gourmet (Erinomainen): Premier culinary mushroom; pan-fry directly in butter with no pre-boiling needed.
  • ** Good Edible (Hyvä): Flavorful, firm edible mushroom; excellent in stews, soups, pies, or dried.
  • * Edible (Syötävä): Mild edible; pleasant culinary filler or best mixed with stronger species.
  • !* Edible Only After Parboiling (Ryöpättävä): Contains acrid or bitter latex/toxins that are completely destroyed or removed by boiling in water for 5–15 minutes and rinsing.

All species in this group have blunt ridges (poimut) or smooth/wrinkled outer surfaces. None have paper-thin, blade-like true gills.

3. Tooth Fungi & Hedgehogs (Orakkaat)

Underneath the cap, these mushrooms produce thousands of fragile, soft, hanging spines (piikit / orakset). In Finland, there are zero poisonous mushrooms with spines underneath the cap!

#Scientific NameEnglish NameFinnish NameChinese NamePartner TreeRatingKey Field Clues
18Hydnum repandumWood HedgehogVaaleaorakas卷缘齿菌 / 白齿菌Spruce, Birch***Cream-buff cap; soft white spines underneath that brush off with a finger; firm brittle chalk-like flesh. 100% bug-free.
19Hydnum rufescensTerracotta HedgehogRusko-orakas红齿菌 / 红褐齿菌Pine, Spruce**Smaller than H. repandum; warm orange-terracotta cap; central stem; fragile spines. Excellent nutty sauté.

6. Traditional Salting Milkcaps (Kirpeät Rouskut - Ryöpättävät)

The backbone of Finland's traditional wild mushroom cuisine. These mushrooms contain sharp, fiery-acrid latex that must be neutralized by parboiling in water (ryöppäys) for 5–15 minutes before curing in salt or sour cream (sienisalaatti).

7. Brittlegills (Haperot / Russula)

Brittle flesh that snaps like chalk; no latex; no ring. Use the Russula Taste Test (Haperosääntö): nibble a pea-sized piece of the cap—if mild and nutty, it is safe to cook; if burning peppery, discard!
